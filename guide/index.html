Planet Fitness East Broadway: PF Black Card

$24.99 a month, $1 to start and a $49 yearly fee. The membership that unlocks HydroMassage, massage chairs, the red-light booth and tanning, at any Planet Fitness.

This is the Planet Fitness membership most people mean when they talk about the massage chairs. At the East Broadway club (7125 E Broadway Blvd, right in 85710) the online offer checked on October 18 and 19, 2026 was $24.99 a month plus tax, a $1 startup fee, a $49 annual fee and no commitment. That offer is marked as expiring November 1.

It is not $29.99 here, at least not yet. Planet Fitness told investors in late 2025 that it planned to raise the Black Card to $29.99 after the 2026 New Year sign-up season. The Tucson clubs checked on October 19, 2026 still list $24.99. If you join now, ask the desk in writing whether your rate is locked if the national price changes; that answer was not published anywhere we could find.

What the Black Card adds over Classic: use of any Planet Fitness club, a free guest every visit, the Black Card Spa (HydroMassage beds, massage chairs, Total Body Enhancement red-light booth, tanning), half-price select drinks, premium app workouts (PF+) and premium partner perks.

Real first-year cost at East Broadway: $1 + (12 × $24.99) + $49 = $349.88 before tax, which works out to about $29.16 a month. That is the number to compare against other gyms, not the $24.99 headline.

Classic gets you the gym floor (cardio, machines, the newer plate-loaded strength equipment and free weights), free small-group training, app workouts and Wi-Fi, but only at your home club. The spa area and guest privileges are Black Card only.

Chuze Fitness 22nd & Kolb: Basic

$9.99 a month with a 12-month commitment ($14.99 without), almost nothing to enroll, but a yearly fee of about $60. The cheapest big-box option in 85710.

Chuze at 7145 E 22nd St is in 85710 and is the cheapest full-size gym nearby. You are right that there is basically no sign-up fee: the enrollment promotion shown on October 19, 2026 was $0.18.

The fine print matters, though. The $9.99 price requires a 12-month commitment; without a commitment Basic is $14.99. Every Chuze membership has an Annual Membership Fee ($59.99 in the site's fine print, shown as $61.55 on the plan cards, which looks like the fee with tax), charged 45 days after you join and then yearly. Paying by credit card adds $2.99 a month; paying from a bank account avoids it.

Real first-year cost on the 12-month Basic plan, paying by bank draft: $0.18 + (12 × $9.99) + $61.55 = $181.61, about $15.13 a month. Without a commitment: $241.61. Leaving a 12-month plan early can cost $58, and Chuze needs 10 days' written notice before a billing date to cancel.

This location also has a Kids Club (the only Chuze in Tucson with one, per its page), 30-plus weekly classes, a turf area, a Lift Lab and tanning. Basic does not include the classes; Premium does.

Planet Fitness fees and fine print

Yes, there is a yearly fee: $49. There is also a startup fee, a bank-account billing rule, an 8-day cancel deadline and a $58 buyout on commitment plans.

Annual fee: $49 a year on both Classic and Black Card at the Tucson clubs we checked. It is billed once a year on your annual fee date. To avoid being charged, your cancellation must reach the club at least 8 days before that date.

Startup fee: varies by club and promotion. At East Broadway it was $1 for Black Card and $19 for Classic. Planet Fitness's own email fine print says memberships may include up to a $49 start-up fee and a 12-month commitment depending on the offer, so make sure the screen you sign on says no commitment.

Billing: many clubs bill from a checking account (EFT) rather than a card. Planet Fitness does not allow partial payments on an overdue balance.

Cancelling: you can cancel by mail to your home club, in person, or online through the member portal. The club must get your notice at least 8 days before your next billing date. If your plan has a minimum term and you end it early, the buyout fee is $58.

What is in the Black Card Spa (and is there cryo?)

HydroMassage, massage chairs, the Total Body Enhancement red-light booth and tanning. No cryotherapy is listed at the Tucson clubs.

HydroMassage: you lie on a padded bed while jets of water move under a waterproof surface. You stay dry and clothed. This is the water massage you described.

Massage chairs: full-body recliner massage chairs, usually on a timer.

Total Body Enhancement: a stand-up booth with red light and a vibration plate, usually a 12-minute session. It is not a sauna; it does not get hot like one. The exact device and light output vary by club and are not published.

Tanning: UV tanning beds or booths. Planet Fitness limits tanning to adults and to no more than once every 24 hours.

Cryotherapy: none of the Tucson club pages list cryotherapy or a cold plunge. Planet Fitness has said it is testing a dry cold plunge and red-light upgrades in select clubs, so ask the East Broadway desk whether anything new is coming.

Rules: phones may not be used for photos, video or calls in the spa area, and amenities can have time and frequency limits.

Will the red light rejuvenate my skin?

Maybe a little, with steady use. Dermatologists say red light may help wrinkles and redness over time, but results are gradual, and gym booths do not publish their dose.

A Cleveland Clinic dermatologist said in September 2026 that, when done consistently, research suggests red-light therapy may help with wrinkles and redness, and may help some people with mild hair loss. She also said not to expect overnight results or dramatic changes.

The gym booth is a general-purpose device. Its exact wavelengths and power are not published, and part of what feels good is the vibration plate, which is not light therapy at all. Think of it as a pleasant extra rather than a skin treatment.

Skip it if you burn or blister easily in sunlight, or take medicines that make you sun-sensitive (some antibiotics and blood-pressure drugs). If skin is the main goal, a dermatologist visit is the better investment.

A word on the tanning beds

Tanning is the one Black Card perk that works against your skin. Dermatologists say there is no safe tanning bed.

The American Academy of Dermatology says there is no such thing as a safe tanning bed, booth or sun lamp. It reports that indoor tanning raises the risk of melanoma by 20%, squamous cell carcinoma by 67% and basal cell carcinoma by 29%, and that wrinkles, age spots and loss of firmness show up years earlier in people who tan.

In Tucson you already get plenty of UV outdoors. If skin rejuvenation is a reason you want the Black Card, use the massage and red-light perks and leave the tanning beds alone.

So, is the Black Card worth it?

If you will actually use the massage chairs and HydroMassage two or more times a week, yes: it is the cheapest way in 85710 to get them, at about $29 a month all-in.

The honest test is use. The Black Card costs about $102 more a year than Classic and about $168 more than Chuze Basic on a 12-month plan. If you use the spa twice a week, that is roughly a dollar or two a visit for a massage-chair or HydroMassage session.

If you mostly want cardio and weights, Chuze Basic or Planet Fitness Classic will do the same job for less. A good plan: use your free Planet Fitness pass and a Chuze guest visit this week, time how often you would really use the spa, and then decide.

Questions to ask before you sign anywhere

Get the startup fee, annual fee date, commitment length, cancel rules and price-lock answer in writing.

  1. What is the total due today, and what will my first two months of statements show?
  2. How much is the annual fee, and on what date is it charged?
  3. Is there a commitment? If yes, how long, and what is the early-cancel fee?
  4. How do I cancel, and how many days before the billing date must you receive it?
  5. Is there a card-processing fee if I do not pay from a bank account?
  6. If your national price goes up, is my rate locked?
  7. Which perks are included in my tier, and are there time limits on them?

Most gyms will waive or cut a startup fee if you ask, especially near the end of a month.

Ask for
Total due today, annual fee date, commitment, cancel notice, card fee, price lock, tier perks
